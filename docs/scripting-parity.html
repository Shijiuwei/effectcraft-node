Expressions and scripting parity

Last reviewed: 2026-10-12 · Last updated: 2026-10-12 · Change: minor (#include / #target removed from the estimate (landed in #759)) · Target: Adobe After Effects 2026

Expressions and ExtendScript are how After Effects work is automated and how much of it is built: rigs, templates, studio pipelines. They are also what carries over from After Effects today, while .aep projects can't. Architecture: architecture.md §5 and §5a; extension points: plugins.md; overall numbers: target-app-parity.md.

Breadth ≈ 93%, ready ≈ 65% (estimated). The object models are written from Adobe's public Expression Language Reference and Scripting Guide (behaviour only, AGENTS.md §2) and tested with our own scripts; no real-world script library has been run against both apps.
